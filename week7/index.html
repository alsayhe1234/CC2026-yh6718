A Living Garden

Week 7

This project extends my previous Flower Clock by adding interactive flowers. The original day-night cycle and mouse-controlled time acceleration remain. Users can click to add flowers, press Backspace to remove the latest flower, or press C to clear the garden. I used a custom `Flower` class and array methods (`push()`, `pop()`, and `splice()`) to manage the flowers. The idea is to explore how time influences nature while human interaction shapes the environment.
